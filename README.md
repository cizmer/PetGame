# PetGame
Project for 2026 FBLA Introduction to Programming

#Main Menu
Where you select you pet and name it. Name field can not be blank. You can pick between a dog, cat, or bunny. 

#Game

## Stats
Your stats are money, level, xp, and the main three: hunger, hygiene, and happiness. Doing good things for your pet will increase its stats. Letting any main stat reach 0 will send the pet to a vet. Where the player must pay a fee. If they can't pay, the game is over and their progress is reset

## Home room
Here you are given the choice to play activities with your pet to increase happiness. 

## Kitchen
Here you can buy food that you can feed your pet. If you feed something it doesn't like, it will have a negative reaction, if it does like it, it will have a positive reaction.

## Bathroom
Where you can bathe your pet
